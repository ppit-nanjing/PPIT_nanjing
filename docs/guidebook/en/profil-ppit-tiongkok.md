## What Is PPIT Tiongkok

PPIT (Perhimpunan Pelajar Indonesia Tiongkok — Indonesian Students Association in China) is a national-level organization that coordinates the community of Indonesian students and scholars throughout China. The organization has its own board, secretariat, and programs.

## Relationship with Branches

PPIT oversees regional branches in various cities, and PPIT Cabang Nanjing is one of them. China-level matters — central correspondence, national programs, and data on other branches — are handled by the central PPIT board, not by PPIT Cabang Nanjing.
