## Signature Dishes of Nanjing

Nanjing has a few dishes that stand as the city's markers. If you only have time to try three, start with these.

- **盐水鸭 Salty Duck (YanShuiYa)** — Nanjing's iconic food, usually eaten in mid-autumn. Salty Duck is usually produced when the Osmanthus flowers are blooming, and the seasonal version is sometimes named **桂花鸭 Osmanthus Duck**. Cuts are often sold per plastic container at specialty duck shops.
- **鸭血汤 Duck Blood Soup** — duck blood soup served with rice vermicelli, one of Nanjing's traditional dishes. This soup is cooked with distinctive spices. Locals believe its ingredients can improve blood circulation, remove toxins, and maintain beauty.
- **黄焖鸡 Huang Men Ji** — a hot plate-style chicken dish with added potato, mushrooms, and vegetables. You can order the spice level to taste, and the dish comes with a bowl of white rice.

Dishes you will also often encounter:

- **汤包 TangBao** — broth and meatballs wrapped in dumpling dough then steamed. Start by biting the skin, drink the broth, then dip the rest in the vinegar provided.
- **锅贴 Guo Tie** — fried dumplings filled with beef and a spice mix, fried until the color turns yellowish.

## Snacks

- **臭豆腐 Chou Dofu** — black-colored fermented tofu that produces a foul smell but tastes delicious. This snack is sold widely at night food spots or roadside kiosks. It is usually accompanied by a broth seasoning with a unique flavor.

Roadside kiosks like this are the easiest place to try it first before deciding whether you like it or not. Small portion, small price.

## Culinary Districts

### 龙眠大道 Longmian Dadao

Longmian is a fairly popular food market area, located beneath the Longmian Da Dao subway station on Line 1.

Metro: **Line 1 Longmiandadao Station**.

### 1912街区 1912 Street

This area is home to world-famous coffee sellers such as Starbucks, the Coffee Beanery, and Kathleen Five, specialty Japanese and Korean restaurants, spas and beauty salons, plus various kinds of bars, pubs, discos, and teahouses.

- Address: 52 Taiping North Rd, Xuanwu District, Nanjing
- Bus: 2, 3, 31, 44, 65, 68, 80, 95, 201, 304, 313, Y2
- Metro: **Line 2 and Line 3 Daxinggong Station Exit 5**

### 狮子桥街区 Shiziqiao Street

Here you can find all kinds of food, including foreign dishes such as Punjabi from India and Japanese Teppanyaki. On this street there are also often various performances presented by locals, so coming at night is livelier.

- Bus: 552, 16, 26
- Metro: Line 1 XuwanwuMen Station, exit toward Hunan Road

## Paying at Places to Eat

The daily currency in China is RMB (人民币), also called CNY or yuan. Daily payments are almost entirely digital via 微信 WeChat and 支付宝 Alipay; carry a little cash as a backup for the first days.
