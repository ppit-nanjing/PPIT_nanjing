## Historical Sites

Nanjing's old sites cluster in two areas: Qinhuai in the south and the Zhongshan Area in the east.

- **夫子庙 Fuzimiao** — free, open daily. Nanjing's core area and the center of Qinhuai River scenery. Address: Qinhuai District, 210001. Bus 37, 7, 166. Metro: **Line 1** 三山街 Sanshanjie Exit 3 or **Line 3** 夫子庙 Fuzimiao Exit 3.

- **中华门 Zhonghua Gate** — ¥50, open 8.30–20.30 daily. Gate and defensive fortress, the old city wall with the most complex structure in the world. Address: Lanqi St, Qinhuai District. Bus 2, 16, 49, 202, 302, 701, 706, y2, y14, y20. Metro: **Line 1** 中华门 Zhonghuamen Exit 2.

- **总统府 Presidential Palace** — ¥40, open 8.00–17.00. Presidential office 1927–1949, now the China Modern History Museum. Address: No. 288 Changjiang Rd, Xuanwu District, 210018. Bus 29, 44, 65, 95, 201, 202, 304. Metro: **Line 3** 大行宫 Daxinggong Exit 5.

- **Nanjing Massacre Memorial Hall** — free, but **bring your passport**. Open 08.30–16.30, Tuesday–Sunday. Memorial museum for the victims of the Nanjing Massacre; at its entrance is the number 300,000. Address: No. 418 Shuiximen St, Jianye District. Bus 7, 37, 166. Metro: **Line 2** 云锦路 Yunjinlu Exit 2.

- **紫金山 ZiJinShan** — free, open 8.30–16.30. Its peak serves as an observatory for viewing the city from above; there is a climbing trail and a gondola. One area with the Zhongshan Area, which contains Linggu Temple and the Dr. Sun Yat-sen Mausoleum (open 8.30–17.00, free, several attractions charge a fee). Address: Zhongshan Park, Xuanwu District. Bus 20, 203, 315; the Zhongshan area is also served by 5, 9, 34, 36, 49, 55, 142, 201, 202, y14, y5. Metro: **Line 2** 苜蓿园 Muxuyuan Exit 1, then continue by bus or 下马坊 Xiamafang.

- **Nanjing Museum** — free, **bring your passport**. Monday 9.00–12.00, Tuesday–Sunday 9.00–17.00. Its permanent collection has more than 400,000 items. Address: No. 321 Zhongshan East Rd, Xuanwu District. Bus 5, 9, 34, 36, 55, 59, 201, 202, y5. Metro: **Line 2** 明故宫 MingGuGong Exit 1.

## Parks and Nature

- **玄武湖 Xuanwu Lake** — free, open 7.00–21.00. Five islands in the lake, connected by arched bridges; there are pagodas, ponds, restaurants, and a small zoo. Address: Xuanwuhu Scenic Spot, Xuanwu District. Bus 1, 25, 28, 30, 33, 35, 47, 201, 304, y1, y2. Metro: **Line 1** 玄武门 Xuanwumen Exit 3.

- **栖霞山 Qixia Shan** — ¥40, open 6.30–17.30. Buddhist temple on Qixia Hill, famous for its maple leaves that turn yellow-red. Address: Qixia St, Qixia District. Bus 362, 138. Metro: **Line 2** 学则路 Xuezelu Exit 2, then bus 138.

- **清凉山公园 Qingliang Mountain Park** — free, open 6.00–18.00. Formerly the summer palace of the Tang Dynasty; best visited in spring and summer. Address: No. 83 Qinliangshan Road, Gulou District. Bus 552, 20, 303, 43, 60, 160, D12. Metro: **Line 7** 清凉山 Qingliang Shan Exit 2.

- **莫愁湖 Mochou Lake** — free, open 6.00–22.00. Many pavilions, gardens, ponds, and rocks, plus rosewood carvings and calligraphy. Address: Shuiximen St, Jianye District. Bus 7, 37, 166, 522, 550, 9, 68, 19. Metro: **Line 2** 莫愁湖 Mochouhu Exit 2.

- **绿博园 Lubo Garden** — free, open 8.30–17.00. Dutch-themed garden; in spring it is full of plums, cherry blossoms, tulips, and violets. Address: Yangzijiang Avenue, Jianye District. Bus 161, D7. Metro: **Line 10** 绿博园 Luboyuan Exit 2.

- **仙林湖 Xianlin Lake** — free, open 8.30–17.00. An alternative to 玄武湖 outside the city, with a different atmosphere. Address: Xianlin University Chengbai Xiangpian Area, Qixia District. Bus 159, 325. Metro: **Line 4** 仙林湖 Xianlinhu Exit 2.

- **红山森林动物园 Hongshan Zoo** — ¥40, open 7.30–16.00. City zoo, has pandas. Address: No. 168 Heyan Road. Bus 8, 30, 54, 64, 72, 209, 501. Metro: **Line 1** 红山动物园 Hongshan Zoo Exit 1.

- **Hexi Ecological Park** — free, open every day. There is a steel bridge crossing the park's waters. Address: intersection of Wuhou Street and Jiangdong South Street Road, Jianye District. Metro: **Line S3** 平良大街 Pingliangdajie Exit 2.

- **Nanjing Yangtze River Bridge** — ¥10, open 7.30–18.00. Double-deck rail bridge connecting Pukou District and Xiaguan. Bus 129, 486, 522. Metro: **Line 10** 绿博园 Luboyuan Exit 1 or 江心洲 Jiangxinzhou Exit 1.

## Shopping and Sightseeing

- **老门东 Laomendong** — free, open every day. Old-town-style area: old buildings, food stalls, and artwork. Address: Jianzi Alley, Qinhuai District. No bus passes through; Metro: **Line 3** 武定门 Wudingmen Exit 2.

- **湖南路 Hunan Road** — free, open 24 hours. Food street with local flavors, plus clothing stores, a market, and restaurants. Address: Gulou District. Bus 522, 16, 26. Metro: **Line 1** 玄武门 Xuanwumen Exit 1.

- **Zifeng Tower** — the central point of the city and the tallest building in Nanjing. From the top you can see 紫金山 to the east, the Yangtze River to the west, Yuhuatai to the south, and Mufu Mountain to the north. Address: No. 1 Zhongshan North Road, Gulou District. Bus 1, 25, 28, 33, 35. Metro: **Line 1 & Line 4** 鼓楼 Gulou Exit 4A.

- **鸡鸣寺 Jimingsi** — ¥30, open 8.00–17.30. A Buddhist temple famous for its bright orange color and the cherry blossom boulevard in spring. Address: Xuanwu District. Bus 68, 304. Metro: **Line 3** 鸡鸣寺 Jimingsi Exit 4.

- **新街口 Xinjiekou** and **百家湖 Baijiahu** — both are on the "Where to go?" list of the campus Nanjing 101 book, but the source doesn't list opening hours, address, or route. Search the name on a map app before departure.

## Notes

This page is for the weekend: one district per day, so you do not lose the day to travel. Historic sites cluster around Qinhuai and the Zhongshan area, and parks are spread across several districts.

Opening hours and ticket prices may change after the guidebook is compiled. Double-check via a map app or the venue's official account, especially during national holidays.

Two free sites still require a passport: Nanjing Massacre Memorial Hall and Nanjing Museum. Bring the original, not a photocopy.

Bus numbers and metro exits are written as-is from the source. If the exit you are heading for is closed, follow the signs inside the station.
