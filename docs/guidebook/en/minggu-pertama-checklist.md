## Seven sequential steps

This sequence is taken from the *Quick Start* page of the guidebook. The first week does not need to be perfect, it just needs to be stable: focus on the order, not on speed.

1. **Secure your passport and core documents.** Your passport, visa, and registration files will be used repeatedly in the early days: campus registration, 住宿登记, opening a bank account, all the way to the medical check-up. Keep everything in one place and leave copies on your phone. The list of documents is in the article **Documents and Files for Arrival** (`dokumen-dan-berkas-awal-datang`). If something goes missing, the first matters are the international student office and the local police station (派出所); the numbers are in **Important Numbers and Contacts** (`nomor-penting-dan-kontak`).
2. **Reach your accommodation safely.** This is the first stable point; other matters wait until the suitcase is in the room. The protocol for arriving past midnight is at the bottom of the section.
3. **Confirm the time and place of registration.** Campus registration has a schedule and location set by the university, and the deadline cannot be moved. The order of campus registration, 住宿登记, and the Residence Permit is in **Visa and Residence Permit** (`visa-dan-izin-tinggal`). If the order is confusing, follow the order given by the university, do not guess on your own.
4. **Make sure your phone can get online.** Maps, chat, apps, all the way to courier calls depend on this connection. The steps from connection to payment, maps, and online transport are in **Apps, Payments, and SIM Cards** (`aplikasi-pembayaran-dan-sim-card`). Payment does not have to be settled on the first day; one working payment method is enough.
5. **Buy or borrow supplies for the first night.** Water, one simple meal, toiletries, and something to sleep on. Do not spend the first night installing every app or chasing a shopping list. Large items are better bought after seeing the room; the principles are in **Housing: Dormitory and Kos** (`tempat-tinggal-asrama-dan-kos`).
6. **Master one route to campus and one route to food.** Just one route per direction, memorize it, do not try all the routes at once. The transport map is in **Transportation Within the City** (`transportasi-dalam-kota`), the food options are in **Culinary and Places to Eat** (`kuliner-dan-tempat-makan`).
7. **Contact the international student office.** From this office you get every school update, including registration schedules and changes. Also contact your lecturer or the teaching office while you have the time: small problems are far easier to sort out before exam season, and teaching assistants (TA) can help with academic matters. The contacts you need to collect are in **Important Numbers and Contacts** (`nomor-penting-dan-kontak`).

## From landing to letting your family know

The order is one-way, not a list you can shuffle:

1. At the airport, follow the immigration and baggage claim signs first, then check your messages. Walking in the right order avoids confusion when you are tired.
2. After picking up your baggage, check that your passport, phone, wallet, and documents are still with you before leaving the arrival area.
3. Before taking transport, make sure of the destination address and save that address in Chinese. This address is used for taxis, ride-hailing, or asking staff. The route from the airport to campus is in **Transport from the Airport to Campus** (`transportasi-dari-bandara-ke-kampus`).
4. At your accommodation, photograph the address, room number, and building entrance. This is what saves you when coming home at night.
5. Send a short arrival message to your family or the school contact so people know you are safe.

### If you arrive after midnight

- **Take the safest direct route** to your first lodging, even if it costs more than the daytime option. Do not move between unfamiliar pickup points.
- **Keep your phone charged** and do not start non-urgent paperwork after midnight.
- **Check in right away**, secure your documents, shower, eat something light, then sleep. Move other matters to the morning.
- **If the dormitory is not ready yet.** If you arrive earlier than scheduled, the dormitory may not be available even though it is booked. Confirm with the school first and prepare temporary lodging or a hotel as a transition.

### After reaching your room

The first four things: make sure where you must report, how to get there, how to stay online, and where your passport and documents are kept.

For the first night, a "good enough" standard is sufficient: passport, phone, wallet, and keys are in one safe place; the time, location, and route for reporting tomorrow are known; water, one simple meal, and sleep essentials are ready; maps and chat work. The rest does not need to be chased that night.

## Focus breakdown

- **Day 1** — arrival, accommodation, rest. Shopping is deferred.
- **Days 2–3** — registration, campus routes, basic connectivity. Non-essential purchases are deferred.
- **First week** — room setup, payments, food orders, package routine. Major upgrades are deferred.
- **First month** — budget, habits, long-term comfort. Optional services are deferred.

Details of first-month costs are in **First-Month Living Costs** (`biaya-hidup-bulan-pertama`).

## The most commonly forgotten things

The four steps above are the easiest to postpone, even though everything else depends on them: confirming the schedule and location of campus registration (step 3), a phone that is truly online (step 4), one campus route and one food route already memorized (step 6), and the contact of the international student office (step 7).

The rest are small matters that easily pile up:

- **Official campus groups.** After registration, join the university's official WeChat or QQ group and save its QR code. Invitation links can expire.
- **Official apps and websites.** For faster and more reliable delivery there is JD (`jd.com`). Other official websites worth saving: `wechat.com`, `alipay.com`, `taobao.com`, `pinduoduo.com`, `goofish.com` (Xianyu), `didi.com`, `amap.com`, `im.qq.com`, `xiaohongshu.com`.
- **Packages and couriers.** If you miss the courier's call, call back or check the message in the delivery app. Don't wait for a package without tracking its status.
- **Your first food order.** Choose a restaurant with many recent reviews and real photos. Write the delivery point clearly in the notes field, and don't stay far from your phone until the order arrives.
- **How to ask for directions.** Show the address or location pin first, then one short question. To the driver or staff, show the address in Mandarin; don't rely on memory. If you get off at the wrong stop, get off safely, check the map, and start again from your current position.
- **Answers at customs.** Answer briefly and honestly, consistent with your documents. If your luggage is delayed, report it before leaving the arrival area, keep the baggage tag and boarding pass, then ask for a case reference number.
