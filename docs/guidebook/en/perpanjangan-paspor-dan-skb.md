## When It Needs to Be Handled

There are two different matters that are often treated as one: passport renewal and the Letter of Study (Surat Keterangan Belajar). Passport renewal is handled when your passport's validity period is running low, whereas the Letter of Study is handled when you need an official document for administrative purposes after returning home.

Neither is handled from Nanjing. The passport is the basic document for almost every matter here, including the Residence Permit and Accommodation Registration 住宿登记, so a passport that is about to expire should be handled first.

## Letter of Study (Surat Keterangan Belajar)

The Letter of Study is needed for degree equivalency in Indonesia. This document is processed at the Embassy of the Republic of Indonesia (KBRI Beijing). The guide source only mentions KBRI Beijing for this service, not KJRI Shanghai, so do not depart for Shanghai if your matter is specifically the Letter of Study.

Requirements for applying for the Letter of Study:

- Photocopy of Surat Verifikasi (Verification Letter).
- Photocopy of passport.

### Diploma Verification

The Surat Verifikasi required as a condition is obtained from the diploma verification process. There are two ways to submit a verification: directly with an appointment that can be arranged by telephone, or online via **siponi.kemdikbud.go.id** and **cqy.chinadegree.cn**.

Information required:

1. Personal information, including full name (given name and Chinese name), gender, date of birth, and ID number (passport number).
2. Study period.
3. Scan of transcript.
4. Copy of proof of payment transfer.

The verification process takes 20 working days. The verification fees: Degree Certificate RMB 260 per sheet, Transcript RMB 360 per sheet, extra copy RMB 50 per sheet. Express verification of 5 working days is subject to an additional RMB 200 per sheet.

The verification process can also be carried out at the **China Academic Degrees and Graduate Education Development Center (CDGDC)**, B-17, TongFang Scientific Plaza No.1 WangZhuang Road, Haidian District, Beijing, 100083, P.R. China.

## Requirements for Passport Renewal

Requirements for applying for passport renewal:

- Online self-reporting at <https://peduliwni.kemlu.go.id>.
- Filling out the passport application form.
- Preparing the original passport and photocopies of all pages of the passport.
- 2 sheets of passport photos with a white background.
- Photocopies of KTP, birth certificate, family card/marriage certificate.
- For those working in China, a letter of employment from the company must be attached along with a photocopy of the work permit.
- For students/pupils, a letter of certification from the school must be attached.

## Application Steps

1. Online self-reporting at <https://peduliwni.kemlu.go.id>.
2. Fill out the passport application form.
3. Prepare the original passport and photocopies of all pages of the passport.
4. Prepare 2 sheets of passport photos with a white background.
5. Prepare photocopies of KTP, birth certificate, and family card/marriage certificate.
6. Attach the letter of employment and photocopy of the work permit if you work in China, or a letter of certification from the school if you are a student/pupil.
7. Submit the complete file to KJRI Shanghai or KBRI Beijing.
