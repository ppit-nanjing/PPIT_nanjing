## From the Airport to the City

Lukou International Airport (南京禄口国际机场) is in Jiangning District, on the southern side of Nanjing. Access to the city uses the Nanjing Metro, getting off at the airport station.

Three options from the terminal:

- **Metro Line S1 (Airport Line)** — get off at 禄口机场 Lukou Jichang Station, exit via Exit 2.
- **Airport Bus** — destination Wanda Plaza.
- **Taxi or DiDi** — for heavy luggage or arriving at night. Save the destination address in Chinese characters on your phone so you can show it to the driver.

S1 meets **Line 1 and Line 3** at 南京南站 Nanjing South Railway Station, so onward journeys to many campuses pass through this station.

## Getting to Campus

Campuses in Nanjing are spread across several areas, for example Jiangning, Xianlin, and Gulou, and their metro routes differ. Use 高德地图 AMap (Gaode Maps) for door-to-door routes, then ask your campus administrator about the routes students usually take.

For the first trip, the metro is easier to follow than the bus: the line numbers are clear, and station names are written in both Chinese characters and pinyin.

## Notes

Fares, bus routes, and operating hours can change. Confirm with the administrator before use.
