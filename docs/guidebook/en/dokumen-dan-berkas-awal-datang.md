## Immigration Documents

Upon arrival, prepare your plane ticket, passport with visa, as well as the Admission Notice and JW201 or JW202. Bring other documents as needed.

## Documents for School Registration

For school registration, the documents to bring:

- JW201 or JW202
- Admission Notice
- Highschool Degree (high school diploma)
- Medical Check-Up, done in China
- CSCA
- University application documents: CV, study plan, personal statement, SKCK, recommendation letter, application form for the university, photocopy of passport, academic transcript, and birth certificate

## Requirements for Passport Extension

Requirements for the passport extension application:

- Online self-report at peduliwni.kemlu.go.id
- Fill out the passport application form
- Original passport and photocopy of every page of the passport
- 2 passport photos with white background
- Photocopy of KTP, birth certificate, family card or marriage certificate
- For those working in China: employment certificate from the company and photocopy of the work permit
- For university students or school students: certificate from the school

Passport extension can be done at the following two places.

Consulate General of the Republic of Indonesia (KJRI Shanghai), Shanghai Mart Building (Office Tower) 16/F Rooms 1607-1608, Yan'an Road West No. 2299, Changning District, Shanghai 200336. Telephone +86-21 5240-2321, 3251-6022.

## Letter of Study

The Letter of Study (Letter of Study) is needed for degree equivalency in Indonesia. Its application requirements are only two: a photocopy of the Verification Letter and a photocopy of the passport. The process is at KBRI Beijing.

The verification submission can be done in two ways: directly with an appointment (can be by telephone) or online via siponi.kemdikbud.go.id or cqy.chinadegree.cn. The information needed:

1. Personal data, including full name (given name and Chinese name), gender, date of birth, and passport number
2. Period of study
3. Scan of transcript
4. Copy of proof of payment transfer

The verification process takes 20 working days. The fees listed in the source: Certificate of Degree RMB 260 per sheet, Transcript RMB 360 per sheet, extra copy RMB 50 per sheet, and express verification (5 working days) with an additional RMB 200 per sheet. The verification process is carried out at CHINA ACADEMIC DEGREES AND GRADUATE EDUCATION DEVELOPMENT CENTER (CDGDC), B-17, TongFang Scientific Plaza No.1 WangZhuang Road, Haidian District, Beijing 100083.

## Notes
