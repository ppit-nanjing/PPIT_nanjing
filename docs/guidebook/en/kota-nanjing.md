## Where is Nanjing

Nanjing is the capital of Jiangsu Province (江苏) in eastern China, and was formerly called Nanking. The city is located not far from Shanghai: about 1 to 1.5 hours by high-speed train.

According to the guidebook, Nanjing is the second-largest city in eastern China with a population of 9,577,000 people. The city lies at the center of the Yangtze River delta (in the source the character printed for this river is 黄河). For hundreds of years Nanjing has been the political and economic center of the Yangtze River delta region, as well as a transportation hub in eastern China and the river estuary area. Its nicknames include "Capital of Science, Technology, Culture, Arts and Tourism" and "Capital of Heaven".

Nanjing is also one of the four ancient capitals of China. The city served as the capital of ten dynasties or kingdoms and of the Republic of China government, recorded from the 3rd century AD until 1949.

For students, Nanjing is known as a student city because of the many famous universities there, making it a destination for students from various regions of China as well as from various countries. Its history and modern life blend together: there are historic buildings and tourist attractions, but also many malls, cafes, parks, and hangout spots.

## Four seasons

Spring (春天). Temperatures range from 28–32 Celsius, the wind is warm, and it is often overcast with the potential for rain. Sources suggest using sunscreen and bringing an umbrella. This season is good for going to parks to see cherry blossoms or plum blossoms, afternoon sports, and strolling. Recommended places: Mochou lake, Xianlin Lake, Lubo Garden, Xuanwu Lake, Mei Hua Shan Festival.

Summer (夏天). The average temperature is 33°C, tends to be scorching, with a slight chance of rain. Sources suggest high-SPF sunscreen, a hat, and cotton clothes. Activities mentioned as suitable: hiking, traveling, working, cleaning the room, or joining a summer camp. Recommended places: Xuanwu Lake, Nanjing Datusha, Qingliang Garden, Yangzte River.

Autumn (秋天). The air is cool or cold with an average temperature of 19°C, the weather tends to be sunny but with a cold breeze. Sources suggest a jacket, handcream, and closed shoes. Recommended places: Ming tomb, Qixia Shan.

Winter (冬天). Temperatures range from 15°C to -10°C, very cold, and the snowy periods are short. Sources suggest a thick, warm jacket and waterproof boots. Activities mentioned as suitable: joining a winter camp and winter holidays. Recommended places: Tangshan hotsprings, Jiangning district, Fuzimiao.
