## Four Seasons

Nanjing has four distinct seasons, and the contrast is huge. Summers are humid and scorching, winter drops close to freezing, while spring and autumn are the most comfortable windows for outdoor activities. That means you need two sets of clothing in one year: one for temperatures above 30°C and one for temperatures below 10°C. Temperature changes between seasons happen fast, so a weekly weather forecast is more useful than your memory of last season.

### Spring 春天

March to May. Temperatures around 8-22°C, with warm gusts of wind. The sky is often overcast with a chance of rain, so a bright morning doesn't mean a bright afternoon. Wear thin layers that are easy to take off, still bring an umbrella, and don't skip sunscreen because the sun is already strong enough at midday.

This season is perfect for going to parks to see cherry blossoms or plum blossoms, afternoon sports, and walks.

- **Mochou Lake** — pavilions, gardens, pond, and stone displays. Open daily 6.00-22.00, free. Metro Line 2, get off at 莫愁湖 Mochouhu Station Exit 2.
- **Xuanwu Lake** — five islands inside the lake connected by arched bridges, there is a pagoda and a small zoo. Open daily 7.00-21.00, free. Metro Line 1, get off at 玄武门 Xuanwumen Station Exit 3.
- **Xianlin Lake** — an out-of-town alternative with a different atmosphere, free entry.
- **Lubo Garden** — Metro Line 10, get off at 绿博园 Luboyuan Station Exit 1.
- **Mei Hua Shan Festival** — plum blossom festival.

### Summer 夏天

June to August. An average of around 28-33°C and it can break past 35°C+, with humid air that makes the mugginess feel heavier. June usually enters the 梅雨 rainy period, so rain falls often before the peak of the heat. Use high-SPF sunscreen, bring a hat, and choose cotton clothes for the scorching heat. You still need to carry an umbrella; afternoon rain often comes without dark clouds first.

Outdoor activities are still possible: hiking, traveling, working, cleaning your room, or joining a summer camp. If possible, shift heavy exercise to the morning or late afternoon.

**Recommended places:** Xuanwu Lake, Nanjing Datusha, Qingliang Garden, Yangzte River.

### Autumn 秋天

September to November. Temperatures around 10-25°C, the air is cool or cold and the weather tends to be sunny. This is the most comfortable season in Nanjing, and also the shortest season. Use a jacket when going out, don't forget hand cream because the air starts to get dry, and wear closed shoes.

An extremely beautiful and romantic autumn, perfect for learning something new like baking or making a scrapbook.

**Recommended places:** Ming tomb, Qixia Shan. At Qixia Temple, the Buddhist temple on Qixia Hill, you can see the scenery of yellow to reddish maple leaves falling. The address is Qixia St, Qixia District.

### Winter 冬天

December to February. Temperatures range from 15°C to -10°C, though the 15°C figure usually only shows up in early December. The weather is very cold, almost freezing, and the snow period is relatively short. Use a thick and warm jacket and waterproof boots. Indoor heating and thermal underwear are lifesavers, because buildings are not always as warm as you'd expect.

A suitable thing to do this season is join a winter camp, and the winter holidays.

**Recommended places:** Tangshan hotsprings, Jiangning district, Fuzimiao. Soaking in hot springs at Tangshan is the most enjoyable way to spend a winter weekend.

## Clothing and Gear

Items you need before the seasons change:

- **High-SPF sunscreen** — used from spring through autumn, not just on beach holidays.
- **Umbrella** — a must for summer and spring. Carry a folding umbrella in your bag.
- **Hat** — for summer.
- **Cotton clothes** — an option for sweltering temperatures.
- **One thin jacket and one warmer layer** — for spring and autumn.
- **A thick, warm jacket** — for winter.
- **Closed-toe shoes** — for autumn.
- **Waterproof boots** — for snow and winter rain.
- **Hand cream** — autumn and winter air dries out your skin.
- **De-humidifier** — for damp places; don't confuse it with a humidifier.

You don't have to buy everything from Indonesia. Winter clothing can be bought after you arrive, and sizes for Indonesian bodies are often easier to find there.

## Notes

The temperatures above are a general overview, not exact figures for every day. Heat waves and cold waves can shift temperatures quite far from that range. Check the forecast before going out, especially in late spring and early autumn when temperatures change quickly.
