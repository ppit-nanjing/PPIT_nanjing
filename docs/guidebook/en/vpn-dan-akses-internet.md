## Why a VPN Is Used

Internet censorship in China blocks some services. Outreach materials and the city guidebook mention VPN as a way to avoid that censorship, while at the same time providing access to blocked social media and messaging apps, for example WhatsApp and LINE. So the VPN matter is not a question of convenience, but of whether or not you can contact family in Indonesia through the apps you normally use.

This setup should be completed before entering China, not after arriving. The city guidebook states firmly: prepare the VPN on your laptop and mobile devices before coming, because VPN provider websites are often blocked in China as well. This means the site where you buy and download the app itself cannot be opened once you are already in Nanjing.

The English version of the guidebook adds a note that the guide does not cover this part for compliance reasons, and advises that these methods or tools not be shared with people you do not know.

## Options Used in the Materials

The services mentioned in the "Pre-Arrival" information session slides are VPNRava/Oppa and LetsVPN. The method shown as an example:

- Open a browser on your laptop, search for **"oppa vpn"** in Chrome.
- Go to <https://rava1.com/>.
- Sign up for an account, then download the file and install the app on your device.
- Buy the **368 RMB for 1 year** plan, **unlimited** quota, for **5 devices**.

The clients available according to the screenshot of that site: Apple iPhone or iPad **iOS 15.0+**, Android phones **Android 7.0+**, Windows PC **Windows 10+**, and Apple Mac **El Capitan (10.11)+**.

Purchase is made through an account on the provider's site: sign up (Sign Up) or log in (Log In), download the client, then choose and pay for a plan. Because the site is blocked in China, all of these steps are done before departure; the prices and payment methods available are listed on each provider's own site.

## What Still Won't Work

- **Without a VPN, WhatsApp and LINE still won't work.** Both are indeed blocked, and that's the main reason a VPN is needed.
- **VPN provider sites can't be relied on from Nanjing.** Re-downloading, forgotten passwords, or buying an add-on plan shouldn't be counted on after you arrive. Save the app and your account details before you leave.
- **This guide doesn't promise any speed or stability of any service.** The choice is yours; the material only lists names, plans, and prices.
