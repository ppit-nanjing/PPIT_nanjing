## Source

The guidebook "A Guide to Nanjing" contains a list of places of worship in Nanjing. The addresses, buses, and metro exits below are copied verbatim from that source.

The mosque list is extended from three online sources, fetched 17 October 2026:

- 南京市民族宗教事务局 (Nanjing municipal religious affairs bureau) — https://mzzjj.nanjing.gov.cn/mzzj/njzjgk/njyslj/
- 南京市伊斯兰教协会 (Nanjing Islamic Association) — http://njislam.com/zjqzs.asp
- China Halal Tours — https://chinahalaltours.com/mosques/contents_type=48

Where sources disagree on a name or address, the government bureau version wins. Each mosque names its source at the end of the line.

## Mosques in Nanjing city

- **净觉寺 Jingjue Mosque** — Sanshan Street, Baixia District, Nanjing, Jiangsu. Bus 37, 7, 166. Metro: **Line 1** 三山街 Exit 3. The Nanjing Islamic Association gives the address as 秦淮区升州路28号 (Jalan Shengzhou No. 28, Qinhuai District), the same as China Halal Tours. The bureau dates the founding to 1388 (洪武二十一年); the association says 1392. ([Open in Amap](https://www.amap.com/place/B00190BC0P))
- **草桥清真寺 Caoqiao Mosque** (also known as 草桥寺) — No. 20 Dading Lane, Qinhuai District, Nanjing, Jiangsu. Bus 28, 16, 302, 33, 100, 35. Metro: **Line 1** 张府园 Exit 3. The bureau places it at 七家湾东首打钉巷 (east end of Qijiawan, Dading Lane) without a house number; China Halal Tours gives 甘雨巷26-32号 (Jalan Ganyu No. 26-32, Qinhuai District). The bureau version is the one used. ([Open in Amap](https://www.amap.com/place/B0FFJ4I694))
- **吉兆营清真寺 Jizhaoying Mosque** (also known as 吉兆营寺) — 玄武区吉兆营43号 (Jalan Jizhaoying No. 43, Xuanwu District), on the east side of Zhongshan Road. The bureau says the mosque was first built around 1770 and rebuilt in 2011–2014, with the new building at over 1,400 m². Bus and metro: the source does not list any. (Sources: religious affairs bureau, Nanjing Islamic Association, China Halal Tours) ([Open in Amap](https://www.amap.com/place/B0FFGCB60X))

## Mosques outside Nanjing city

- **凤凰花园清真寺 Fenghuangshan Garden Mosque** (Amap: 澄清坊清真寺) — No. 30 Changjiang Road, Xiongzhou Subdistrict, Luhe District (长江路30号). Bus 501, 503. Metro: **Line S8** 雄州 Exit 4. The Nanjing Islamic Association also records this address as 长江路清真寺. ([Open in Amap](https://www.amap.com/place/B00190AI9M))
- **六合南门清真寺 Nanmen Mosque (Luhe)** (also known as 南门寺) — 六城镇南门清真街北首, north end of Nanmen Qingzhen Street, Luhecheng (njislam names the town, not the district), facing the Chuhe River (门前临滁河). Founded 1404 (明永乐二年), originally 5,000 m² with 38 rooms. Bus and metro: the source does not list any. (Source: Nanjing Islamic Association) ([Open in Amap](https://www.amap.com/place/B0FFMA5PNG))
- **六合竹镇清真寺 Zhuzhen Mosque** (also known as 王家巷清真寺) — Also called Wangjia Lane Mosque. Located in Zhuzhen town, Luhe District; the source does not list a house number or street name. Founded 1901 (清光绪二十七年), three courtyards (north, south, east), around 1,200 m². Bus and metro: the source does not list any. (Source: Nanjing Islamic Association) ([Open in Amap](https://www.amap.com/place/B0FFGILCCX))
- **江宁湖熟清真寺 Hushu Mosque** (also known as 水北清真寺) — 江宁区湖熟镇水北街39号 (Jalan Shuibei No. 39, Hushu town, Jiangning District), per China Halal Tours. The Nanjing Islamic Association writes 湖熟镇水北繁华街 and also calls it Shuibei Mosque. Built in the 明洪武 era, three west-facing courtyards; the gate and side rooms were rebuilt in 1919–1920. Bus and metro: the source does not list any. (Sources: Nanjing Islamic Association, China Halal Tours) ([Open in Amap](https://www.amap.com/place/B0FFG77EWC))
- **溧水清真寺 Lishui Mosque** (also known as 小西门寺 / 魏家塘寺) — Located in the 魏家塘 area at 小西门 (Weijiatang, Small West Gate), Lishui District; the source does not list a house number. The old mosque was three rooms of about 400 m²; it was relocated and replaced with a two-storey building in 2009–2010. Bus and metro: the source does not list any. (Source: Nanjing Islamic Association) ([Open in Amap](https://www.amap.com/place/B0FFH89J7W))
- **浦口星甸清真寺 Xingdian Mosque** — 浦口区星甸 (Xingdian, Pukou District); the source does not list a street or house number. Founded 1741 (清乾隆六年), rebuilt in 2011. Bus and metro: the source does not list any. (Source: Nanjing Islamic Association) ([Amap route](https://uri.amap.com/search?keyword=%E6%B5%A6%E5%8F%A3%E6%98%9F%E7%94%B8%E6%B8%85%E7%9C%9F%E5%AF%BA&city=%E5%8D%97%E4%BA%AC&src=ppit-nanjing))
- **浦口江浦清真寺 Jiangpu Mosque** — 江浦街道 (Jiangpu Subdistrict, Pukou District); the source does not list a street or house number. Founded around 1929, renovated in 2008 to 370 m². Bus and metro: the source does not list any. (Source: Nanjing Islamic Association) ([Open in Amap](https://www.amap.com/place/B0GKS7M9T4))
- **一条巷清真寺 Yitiaoxiang Mosque** — No. 9 Yitiao Lane, Zhujiang town, Pukou District, Nanjing. The source does not list a Mandarin name; the mosque takes its name from the lane it sits on. Covers 160 m², built before 1929, returned to the Pukou District Islamic Association in 2001 and repaired seven years later. Bus and metro: the source does not list any. (Source: China Halal Tours) ([Amap route](https://uri.amap.com/search?keyword=%E4%B8%80%E6%9D%A1%E5%B7%B7%E6%B8%85%E7%9C%9F%E5%AF%BA&city=%E5%8D%97%E4%BA%AC&src=ppit-nanjing))

## Practical notes

- Houses of worship for all major religions are easy to find in large cities like Nanjing: temples, churches, and mosques are available.
- Around 25% of China's population practices a religion, so it is no problem if you have your own beliefs.
- Respect other people's beliefs. If someone offends your beliefs, or if your beliefs need to be mentioned in official matters, ask for help from the school or an official institution. The campus international student office is the easiest first step.
- Each location above mentions both bus and metro. The metro exit listed is the closest one to the station; after exiting, the rest of the trip is usually on foot or continues by the bus listed.
- The mosques added from the bureau site, the Nanjing Islamic Association, and China Halal Tours come without bus or metro exits. If you want to go, check the route on each mosque's Amap link.
- The Amap links above are name searches, not coordinates. Amap uses the GCJ-02 system, so there is no lat/lng here.
- Double-check prayer times and service schedules before setting out. The source guidebook does not mention the language of the services (Mandarin, English, or both), so ask the house of worship's administrators directly or the campus international student office.
