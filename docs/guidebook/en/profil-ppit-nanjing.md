## What PPIT Nanjing Branch Is

PPIT Nanjing Branch is a student organization in Nanjing. This organization serves as a forum for Indonesian students and university students, as well as Indonesian student organizations in Tiongkok, particularly in Nanjing.

The organization was founded on 28 October 2008, coinciding with Sumpah Pemuda Day, under the name PERMIT Nanjing. The name later changed to Perhimpunan Pelajar Indonesia Tiongkok Cabang Nanjing, or PPIT Nanjing. The guidebook drafting team notes that this organization also played a key role in the founding of PPI Tiongkok.

The nature of the organization is described as open, grounded in the spirit of brotherhood and nationalism. PPIT Nanjing Branch does not take sides and is not tied to any political organization, is oriented toward social work, and does not prioritize profit. The organization also strives to improve the academic capacity and potential of its members.

## PPIT headquarters and other branches

PPIT Nanjing Branch is not the head office. PPIT (Perhimpunan Pelajar Indonesia Tiongkok) is a separate Tiongkok-level organization, with its own board, secretariat, and programs; Nanjing is one of its branches. Tiongkok-level matters (headquarters correspondence, national programs, data on other branches) should not be addressed to PPIT Nanjing.

- PPIT official website: <https://www.ppitiongkok.com/>.
- Branch directory: open the PPIT website and press the **View All Branches** button. There you will find a list of branches by province and city along with the contacts and social media of each branch.
- PPIT social media (headquarters, not branch channels): [Instagram @ppitiongkok](https://www.instagram.com/ppitiongkok/), [X @ppi_tiongkok](https://x.com/ppi_tiongkok), [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/PerhimpunanPelajarIndonesiaTiongkok), and [YouTube @PPITiongkok](https://www.youtube.com/@PPITiongkok).
- The social media of each branch, including Nanjing, is in the branch directory (the **View All Branches** button), not in the list of headquarters channels above.
- The Nanjing branch contact listed in the PPIT directory: ppitnanjing.partnership@gmail.com (External Secretary).
- The website address of each branch follows the pattern `https://www.<nama-cabang>.ppitiongkok.com`. Branch subdomains are being prepared; while they are not yet active, use the branch directory on the PPIT website.

## Who is covered

At the time the guidebook was compiled, PPIT Nanjing Branch covered more than 600 Indonesian students and university students spread throughout Nanjing.

In addition to Nanjing, PPIT Nanjing also covers Indonesian students and university students in other cities, namely Xuzhou, Jurong, Ma'anshan, Zhenjiang, and Huai'an.

There are two active campus-level organizational branches:

- INA (Indonesian NUIST Association) at NUIST (Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology), Nanjing.
- JIA (JSAHVC Indonesian Association) at JSAHVC (Jiangsu Agri-animal Husbandry Vocational College), Taizhou.

## What is done for Indonesian students

The form of work documented in the source is the publication of the guidebook "A Guide to Nanjing". This book was compiled by a drafting team to make it easier for Indonesian students to find important information about Nanjing.

The contents of the guidebook cover a brief profile of PPIT Nanjing Branch, transportation information, public places, places of worship, tourist attractions, and other information needed by students while studying in Nanjing. In the closing section of the book, PPIT Nanjing signs the page with the name of its organization.

The sources used for this article do not list official contact channels for PPIT Nanjing, such as WeChat, Instagram, or organizational email. For branch matters, use the contact published by PPIT in the section "PPIT headquarters and other branches"; for Tiongkok-level matters, use the PPIT channels on its official website.
