## Bus

Nanjing buses reach the city center, the outskirts, and mountain areas. Routes are marked with color codes: blue, red, and green.

Fares are divided by zone:

| Zone | Fare |
| --- | --- |
| City center | 2 RMB |
| Outside the city | 3–4 RMB |
| Suburbs | 3–5 RMB (sometimes calculated by distance) |

How to pay: cash, Transportation Card, or Alipay. If you ride the bus often, the Transportation Card is more economical because bus fares are cheaper.

## Metro

Metro lines are distinguished by color: **Line 1 blue**, **Line 2 red**, **Line 3 green**, and so on.

Metro fares are calculated by distance, ranging from **RMB 2 to RMB 9**. Tickets can be bought with cash or using a Subscription Card.

**Subscription Card** can be bought and topped up at stations. This card is valid for the metro and bus, and bus fares become cheaper if you use it often.

Maps and route information are at [www.nj-dt.com](http://www.nj-dt.com).

## Bikes

Colorful shared bikes — yellow, blue, gray — are available in public areas. Rental is only through a dedicated app, and the app used depends on the color of the bike.

- **Deposit:** 199 RMB for the subscription card.
- **Fare:** 2 RMB per hour, deducted directly from the deposit.

## Taxi

Taxi fares are calculated by distance traveled. There is an additional fuel surcharge of **2 RMB**.

Online taxis are widely used because they are affordable and comfortable. Order via the app, then show a clear pickup point and destination.

## High-Speed Rail and Airplanes

Nanjing is strategically located for traveling to other cities. The favorite city of students here is Shanghai, **301 km** from Nanjing.

High-speed rail and airplane tickets can be bought via the WeChat mini program, **携程旅行** Ctrip, or the international app **Trip.com**.

**高铁 High-Speed Rail**

High-speed trains have code prefixes G, D, or C, and their maximum speed is 350–400 km per hour. Nanjing has two main stations:

- **南京站 Nanjing Zhan** — in the northeast.
- **南京南站 Nanjing Nan Zhan** — in the south of the city center.

**飞机 Airplane**

Lukou International Airport (南京禄口国际机场) is in the southern part of Nanjing. Access is via Nanjing Metro **Line S1**, getting off at the airport station 禄口机场 Lukou Jichang Station.

## How to Ride the Metro

1. **Enter the station.** Tap your card or ticket coin, or use the Alipay app at the entrance gate.
2. **Read the guide boards.** Boards inside the station show the routes and the direction of the trains. Choose the route that matches your destination, then wait on the correct platform.
3. **Let others out first, then get in.** When the train arrives, let passengers out first, then look for an empty seat if there is still one.
4. **Watch the announcements.** Boards inside the train show the next station. Most stations are announced in Mandarin and English.
5. **Exit the station.** Scan the barcode in the Alipay app to open the exit gate. The fare charged is also visible in the app.

## Buying Metro Coins 地铁币

地铁币 (dìtiě bì) is a one-way metro coin. This coin is used if you don't yet have a metro payment service like Alipay, or when your phone is out of battery so you can't pay online.

- **Find the machine.** Every metro station provides metro coin vending machines. Payment can be online or cash.
- **Select the destination station.** You cannot exit the station if the destination that comes out doesn't match the one you selected.
- **Pay.** If using Alipay, make sure your account is already linked to a bank account. If using cash, prepare 1 RMB, 5 RMB, or 10 RMB denominations.
- **Use the coin.** Scan the coin to enter the station. To exit, insert the coin into the slot next to the scanner.

## Notes

Fares and operating hours can change. Check the map app or the official metro website before traveling.
