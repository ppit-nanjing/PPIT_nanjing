## Sequence from Arrival to Residence Permit

The usual order of paperwork that new students handle after arriving in China:

1. Report at immigration. Bring your plane ticket, passport with visa, as well as the Admission Notice and JW201 or JW202.
2. Registration at the university.
3. Medical check-up (MCU).
4. Buy insurance.
5. Open a bank account.
6. Temporary residence registration (住宿登记).
7. Apply for the Residence Permit.

After arriving in China, you have a maximum of 30 days to apply for the Residence Permit.

For insurance, the purchase is made through the WeChat account 来华保服务 by filling in your personal data. Payment is 800 RMB per year and only WeChat or Alipay is accepted, you cannot use an international bank card such as Visa or Mastercard. The type of insurance can differ depending on the campus.

## Registration at the University

Documents that need to be prepared: passport, Admission Notice, JW201 or JW202, high school diploma, and CSCA. The rest follows the regulations of each campus.

## X1 Student Visa Documents

To apply for a student visa at the Chinese embassy or consulate, NUIST lists four documents: the NUIST Admission Notice, the original copies of the Visa Application for Study in China (JW201 or JW202), the Foreigner Physical Examination Form, and the blood test report.

The length of study determines the visa type. Studying more than 6 months requires an X1 visa, and the visa is valid for 30 days from the date of entry into China. Less than 6 months can use an X2 visa; the stay follows what is written on the visa and no residence permit is needed, unless your study period is extended. Registration and payment of school fees are done before applying for the residence permit, and do not enter China too early.

## Medical Check-Up

The MCU is done in China. The steps are:

1. Make an appointment. Follow the official WeChat account 江苏国际旅行卫生保健中心, select the menu 业务预约, then choose the date and time.
2. Come according to the schedule. Bring your passport, 2 sheets of 3×4 photos with a white background from the last 6 months, 600 RMB for the examination fee, and the MCU results from the last 6 months if you already have them.
3. Collect the results. You can choose the physical or online form. Physical results are sent to the address you filled in within 3–5 days, online results come out within a few hours through the WeChat account.

## Open a Bank Account

Documents: passport, Admission Letter (录取通知书), JW201 or JW202, and a letter of statement from parents for those under 18 years of age. The card must be made in person and cannot be represented by someone else. Once made, the card takes around 5–7 days to become active and can only be used after activation is complete.

## Temporary Residence Registration (住宿登记)

Registration can be done yourself through the 我的南京 app in the Temporary residence registration menu, select the 外国人 category. The campus can also help, depending on each campus.

An example of doing it yourself from Nanjing University, through WeChat:

1. Search for 南京公安.
2. Click 微服务.
3. Click 境外人员临时住宿登记.
4. Fill in the data, click submit, then wait a few hours.

## Temporary Residence Registration: 24-Hour Deadline and Documents

The deadline is the same on every route: 24 hours after you move into a new place or change address. The Nanjing city rules refer to Article 39 of the Exit and Entry Administration Law. If you stay at a hotel, the hotel handles the registration for you.

There are three official routes:

1. Go to the comprehensive service window at the local police station.
2. Follow the WeChat account Nanjing Public Security (南京公安), select the microservice menu, then 境外人员临时住宿登记.
3. Download the My Nanjing app, then open Exit and Entry Government Service.

NAU (南京审计大学) lists the 南京公安 app as the online registration channel, with an extra note: students who are going to change or extend their residence permit must register again first. The rule is the same at NJUST, which also names the form, 临时住宿登记表.

Documents to bring according to NUIST:

- Original passport
- Photocopies of the personal information page, photo page, valid visa or residence permit page, and entry stamp page
- A completed Temporary Accommodation Registration Form

NJU students living in campus dorms do not need to go to the police station themselves because the dorm administrator reports your address. Those living off campus still have to report in person.

## Residence Permit

Documents to prepare: passport and a photocopy of the passport information page, Admission Notice (录取通知书), JW201 or JW202, medical check-up results, Accommodation Registration Form, a letter from the university, 3 passport photos with a white background, a photocopy of the visa, and a photocopy of the last entry stamp.

Make an appointment through 我的南京, in the Exit and Entry service (出入境) select 办证预约 (Visa For Foreigners), then determine the location, day, and time. Come according to the appointment with the original documents. The passport is left at the office during the processing. After the passport is handed over, you receive a passport collection slip that contains the latest date the residence permit is issued. Keep that slip because it is needed when collecting the passport. The fee is 400 RMB per year. The passport is collected according to the date on the slip or can be checked through the official WeChat account 江苏出入境.

## Additional Documents According to NJUST

At NJUST there are several document requirements not always mentioned by other campuses: the yellow page (黄联) of JW201 or JW202, one passport-size photo 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm with a white background, and transfer approval or a graduation certificate from the previous Chinese university if you are a transfer student. The form is 《外国人签证、居留许可申请表》.

## Residence Permit Duration by Scholarship Type (NJUST)

At NJUST, the length of the residence permit follows your scholarship type or how your study is funded:

- Chinese Government Scholarship — follows the registered study duration.
- Jasmine Jiangsu Government Scholarship and Nanjing Municipal Government–NJUST Joint Scholarship — handled one year at a time, so you renew every year.
- Self-supported students — follows how long your tuition and accommodation fees have been paid for. The stay cannot exceed the registered study duration.
- Students under a training agreement (委托培养) — follows the period stated in the agreement.

## The SIE Does Not Handle Your Paperwork (NJUST)

At NJUST, the SIE (国际教育学院) does not handle temporary lodging registration, residence permit application, extension or alteration on your behalf. You handle all of it yourself at 南京市公安局出入境管理支队, and you pay the handling fees directly there. The SIE only issues the supporting letters you need.

## Health Certificate

A Health Certificate from Jiangsu International Travel Health Care Center is required for the residence permit application, but when it is mandatory differs between campuses:

- NUIST — for students over 18 years old whose study duration is more than one academic year.
- NJUST — for applicants over 18 years old whose study duration is more than 6 months, or cumulatively more than 6 months.
- NJU — residence permits valid for less than 12 months do not require a health examination, while 12 months or longer do. A second residence permit application always requires one, and if the Health Certificate was not submitted with the first application, it has to be submitted at the extension.

Verification is done at Jiangsu International Travel Healthcare Center. If your documents do not meet the requirements, you undergo another examination first and only receive the Health Certificate after passing it.

## Health Examination Fees

There are two different rates, depending on whether you already have examination results from your home country:

- Verification of an overseas examination result — 60 RMB per person, according to NUIST and NJU. Bring the original Physical Examination Record for Foreigner plus the HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis report; extra examinations such as a blood test may be requested.
- Full examination in China — 430 RMB per person according to NUIST, 450 RMB according to the NJU guide. Come on an empty stomach and bring your original passport plus 2 passport-size photos.

Service hours also differ. NUIST lists examination time as 8:30–11:00 Monday–Friday. NJU lists examination time as 8:00–11:00 and verification time as 8:00–11:30 and 13:30–17:00, Monday–Friday.

## Residence Permit Fee

The figures in the campus guides are not uniform. The NJU booklet "Welcome Guide for International Exchange Students" says a residence permit application normally costs 600–800 RMB, while the figure commonly used is 400 RMB per year. Prepare extra funds from the start and confirm the exact amount at the counter, because the fee can change.

## Extension and Alteration Deadlines

Deadlines differ by campus and permit type:

- NJUST — apply for an extension or alteration at the SIE at least 10 working days before the residence permit expires, so that the SIE has time to issue the supporting letters.
- NJU — apply at the Visa Affairs Office at least one month before the residence permit expires.
- Nanjing city rules — if the duration of stay on your visa is 180 days or less, apply for an extension at least 7 days before the stay period ends. If you already hold a residence permit, apply for an extension at least 30 days before it expires.

What is the same everywhere: keep watching the expiry dates of your passport, visa, and residence permit, and do not wait for a reminder.

## Data Changes Must Be Reported Within 10 Days

If your passport number, residential address, place of study, or accompanying family members (偕行人) change, NJUST gives a 10-day deadline from the date of change to apply for the alteration at 南京市公安局出入境管理支队. NJU uses the same deadline for students who move, with two routes: NJU for X1 holders and the nearest police station for X2 holders.

## Overstay Penalties

According to the NJUST rules, which refer to national regulations, staying past the permit's expiry is penalised in stages:

- A warning, for minor violations.
- If considered serious, a fine of 500 RMB per day of illegal stay, capped at 10,000 RMB in total.
- Alternatively, detention of 5 to 15 days.

The office also has the right to reject visa or residence permit applications, and to revoke or declare void permits already issued.

## Lost Passport or Residence Permit

The procedure according to the Nanjing city guide if a passport is lost or stolen:

1. Report it to the local police and ask for the case evidence.
2. Register at 南京市公安局出入境管理分局. After investigation, they issue the Certificate of Missing Passport.
3. Apply for a new passport at the embassy or consulate of your home country in China.
4. Once the new passport is ready, handle the visa and residence permit at the 出入境 office.

NJUST adds one step for a lost residence permit: bring a letter from the SIE to the 出入境 office, then apply for a replacement. Passports, visas, and residence permits must not be smeared or damaged.

## Work, Internships, and Driving Licences

A residence permit with the reason of study is not a work permit. A few rules worth knowing:

- Under the Nanjing city regulations, foreigners who work must hold a work permit and a working residence permit. No party may employ a foreigner without both.
- NJUST states that a residence permit with the reason "学习" cannot be used to work. For off-campus work-study or an internship, get approval from the SIE first, then ask the 出入境 office to have the location and duration annotated in your residence permit. Without that annotation, the internship is prohibited.
- An international driving licence is not valid in Nanjing. To drive, you must pass the exam at the Nanjing Public Security Bureau Traffic Management Department and obtain a Chinese licence.

## Notes

Immigration rules and fees can change, and this guide may lag behind the latest situation on the ground. Confirm with the administrators or the university's international affairs office before handling the paperwork.
